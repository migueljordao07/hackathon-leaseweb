# Hackathon Portugal 2026 — Challenge & Guidelines

**InfraHack: AI for Cloud Infrastructure** · Leaseweb × Velv
Dehouse Picoas, Lisbon, Portugal · 20 November 2026

> Source: `Hackathon Portugal 2026 - Challenge & guidelines overview 1 Finalito.pdf`

## The Challenge

Use AI to make cloud infrastructure smarter, safer, and more efficient. We're looking for innovative solutions around cloud operations, security, data protection, migration, and hyperscaler alternatives.

You can make anything you like, as long as it follows the theme above. Themes that are particularly interesting for the Jury are:

- **AI-assisted cloud infrastructure** — AI is making our lives easier, and can help in building, maintaining and using Cloud infrastructure as well. Create an interesting tool or use case for an AI assisted Cloud Infrastructure topic, and show it in a demo.
- **Hyperscaler alternatives / sovereignty** — Europe is often (too) locked in to hyperscalers, but we do have the skills and platforms to take back control. Showcase tooling to create an alternative for a hyperscale service that is locking you in, or make migrating to a sovereign alternative easier.
- **Security** — It seems that new and very severe security issues and exploits are being released almost every day now. Can we make smarter tools to help spot, mitigate or prevent them, possibly using AI? Create something that showcases a smart and creative new way of dealing with data or cloud security.

## Guidelines

### Before you start

Make sure you have:

- An active GitHub account for you and your team
- A development and presentation/demo environment available
- We will provide one Linux virtual machine per team to use. These will be decommissioned after the event!

### Submission

The result of your project should be:

- **In Git:**
  - A working version of your project, in a public git repo, ready to be checked out
  - Instructions to build and run your project
  - A concise `README.md` describing your project, the goals, and the structure
  - The repository should be in a publicly reachable repository that remains available
- **On the day:**
  - A presentation and/or demo showcasing your result
  - Live demos are appreciated, but their functioning will be part of the scoring

## Evaluation Criteria and Rules

### Evaluation criteria and scoring

The judges will score on a scale of 1 to 5 in the following categories:

- **Innovation:** originality, creativity, and the relevance of solutions submitted.
- **Applicability:** consider real-world potential, ideally demonstrated through a prototype or demo.
- **Technical complexity:** quality will be taken into account, along with implementation and use of AI and cloud infrastructure.
- **Presentation & clarity:** there should be a demonstration of an ability to explain the problem, solution, technical approach, and impact.

All criteria have the same weight and winners will be selected based on their total amount of points. The judges' result is final, and scores from individual judges will not be disclosed.

### Rules

- Open-source frameworks are allowed as long as they are publicly available and included in your build instructions / dependencies.
- Clear documentation on building, usage and setup should be included.
- Your pitch can be **5 minutes maximum**.
- Choice of language is open, but common languages are strongly preferred (i.e. Python, Golang, PHP).
- Your project should have an open license (i.e. Apache and the like) and the license should allow non-commercial and commercial re-use. Make sure you are OK to work on an open/non-commercial project!
- You start from scratch, on the day.
